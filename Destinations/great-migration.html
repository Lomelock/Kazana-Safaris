Your Journey

Day by Day

1 Arrival & Western Serengeti

Arrive at Kilimanjaro and connect to your charter flight to the western Serengeti, where the migration gathers. Settle into your lodge positioned for optimal viewing of the herds. Evening game drive introduces you to the mass movements and predators following the action.

Flight Western Serengeti Migration
2 Migration Tracking

Full day following the migration in real-time. Your guide uses knowledge of herd movements to position you for epic encounters. Witness millions of wildebeest and zebras creating dust clouds as they move across the plains. Predators circle — lions, hyenas, and wild dogs hunting the weak and young.

Wildebeest Zebras Predators Herd Dynamics
3 River Crossing Quest

The crown jewel of the Great Migration — the Grumeti and Mara River crossings. Position yourself for the dramatic moment when millions surge across crocodile-infested waters. Witness nature's raw drama as predators feast and the herd presses forward, driven by instinct.

River Crossing Crocodiles Drama Primal Nature
4 Central Serengeti Safari

Move deeper into the Serengeti's central regions where the migration concentrates around water sources. Multi-day game drive tracking lions, cheetahs, and other predators capitalizing on the abundance. Your naturalist guide explains the ecological story unfolding around you.

Central Serengeti Big Cats Game Drive Ecology
5 Predator & Prey Dynamics

Spend another full day immersed in the Serengeti's predator-prey relationships. Follow prides of lions hunting buffalo, watch cheetahs pursue gazelles, and observe wild dogs on the hunt. Evening drives often yield unique nocturnal encounters — hyenas, jackals, and nocturnal birds.

Lions Cheetahs Wild Dogs Hunt Sequences
6 Northern Serengeti Exploration

Venture to the northern Serengeti as herds continue their annual cycle toward the Masai Mara in Kenya. Game drive through rolling hills and acacia forests, tracking remaining migration herds. This remote region offers intimate wildlife encounters far from crowds.

Northern Parks Remote Serengeti Intimate Encounters Acacia Forests
7 Final Safari & Reflections

One last full day in the Serengeti to absorb all you've witnessed. Morning and afternoon game drives capture any wildlife moments you may have missed. Reflect on the incredible ecological cycle you've been privileged to observe firsthand.

Final Safari Reflection Last Encounters Gratitude
8 Departure

Final morning in Africa — time permitting, one last sunrise game drive through the endless plains. Transfer to the airstrip for your charter flight to Kilimanjaro and onward journey home. You'll depart with indelible memories of Earth's greatest wildlife spectacle.

Sunrise Last Moments Departure
