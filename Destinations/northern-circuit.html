Your Journey

Day by Day

1 Arrival & Tarangire

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport and connect to your charter flight to Tarangire National Park. After settling into your boutique lodge, embark on an evening game drive to start your adventure. Witness the Tarangire River's legendary wildlife as the sun sets over the plains.

Flight Tarangire Sunset Drive
2 Tarangire Deep Dive

Full day in Tarangire National Park, home to thousands of elephants and the densest concentration of wildlife outside the Serengeti. Explore various ecosystems — baobab forests, grasslands, and river corridors. Your expert guide will position you for close encounters with lions, giraffes, zebras, and countless bird species.

Big Five Elephant Herds River Views Birdwatching
3 Serengeti Arrival

Morning game drive in Tarangire before your transfer to the Serengeti. Fly by charter to the Serengeti's central hub at Seronera. Afternoon game drive through the endless plains, where predators and prey engage in nature's ancient dance. Your lodge sits within the park for an authentic wilderness experience.

Flight Serengeti Predators Plains
4 Serengeti to Ngorongoro

Full day safari in the Serengeti, timing your movements with wildlife migrations and predator activity. Afternoon transfer to the Ngorongoro Crater rim. This UNESCO World Heritage Site is a natural wonder — a massive caldera containing one of the highest concentrations of wildlife on Earth.

Migration Game Drive Crater Rim UNESCO Site
5 Crater & Departure

Descend into the Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable morning safari. The crater floor hosts a self-contained ecosystem with rhinos, lions, hyenas, and vast buffalo herds. Lunch at your lodge before the charter flight back to Kilimanjaro and your onward journey home.

Crater Floor Rhinos Endemic Species Departure
