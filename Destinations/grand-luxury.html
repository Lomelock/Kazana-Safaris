Your Journey

Day by Day

1 Arrival & Serengeti

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport and connect to your charter flight to the Serengeti. Settle into your luxury lodge and enjoy an afternoon game drive through the endless plains. Watch the African savanna come alive as the sun paints the horizon gold.

Flight Serengeti Sunset
2 Serengeti Wildlife Safari

Full day immersed in the Serengeti's legendary wildlife theater. Morning and afternoon game drives with your expert guide track predators, herbivores, and the intricate web of savanna life. Experience why this vast ecosystem is considered one of the world's greatest natural wonders.

Big Cats Migration Game Drives Wildlife
3 Ngorongoro Crater

Morning game drive in the Serengeti before transfer to Ngorongoro Crater. Descend into this UNESCO World Heritage Site, a massive natural amphitheater containing one of Earth's highest wildlife concentrations. Witness rhinos, lions, elephants, and buffalo in their self-contained ecosystem.

UNESCO Site Crater Floor Rhinos Endemic Species
4 Ngorongoro Deep Dive

Spend another full day exploring the Ngorongoro Crater's diverse habitats — forests, grasslands, and soda lakes. Your guide will reveal the unique Maasai cultural presence and the crater's fascinating geology. Late afternoon, transfer to the Arusha region for your next adventure.

Crater Safari Geology Cultural Soda Lakes
5 Zanzibar Arrival

Fly from Arusha to Zanzibar, trading the savanna for turquoise waters and white sand beaches. Arrive at your luxury beachfront resort and have the afternoon to settle in, swim, and acclimate to island time. Dinner overlooking the Indian Ocean marks the transition from bush to beach.

Flight Beach Ocean Resort
6 Zanzibar Island Adventures

Explore the magic of Zanzibar — the Spice Island. Visit historic Stone Town with its winding streets and Swahili architecture, snorkel pristine coral reefs teeming with tropical fish, or relax on powder-soft beaches. Optional sunset dhow cruise through coral gardens completes your day.

Stone Town Snorkeling Spice Tours Dhow Cruise
7 Departure

Your final morning on Zanzibar — swim, walk the beach, or enjoy a spa treatment at your resort. Afternoon transfer to the airport for your flight home, carrying memories of African wilderness and tropical paradise combined.

Beach Time Relaxation Departure
