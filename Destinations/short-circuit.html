Your Journey

Day by Day

1 Arrival

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport and connect to your charter flight to Tarangire National Park. Settle into your lodge with time to relax and acclimate. In the late afternoon, embark on your first game drive as the golden hour illuminates the park.

Flight Game Drive Lodge
2 Tarangire & Lake Manyara

A full day of exploration. Morning game drive in Tarangire, home to thousands of elephants and the densest concentration of wildlife in Tanzania. The Tarangire River is a lifeline during the dry season, gathering vast herds. Afternoon transfer to Lake Manyara, where flamingos dust the alkaline waters pink and baboons crowd the cliffs.

Elephants Lions Flamingos Scenic Beauty
3 Departure

Early morning game drive at Lake Manyara as wildlife emerges in the cool light. Return to your lodge for breakfast and a rest. Transfer to the airstrip for your charter flight back to Kilimanjaro, departing with memories of intimate encounters with Africa's most iconic animals.
